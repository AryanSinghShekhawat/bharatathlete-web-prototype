How to use
  1. Allow camera access on your phone.
  2. Enter height & hit Calibrate (stand straight).
  3. Hit Start Test and perform test; then Stop.
  4. Save result & press Sync to simulate upload.
Tip: Use Chrome on Android for best compatibility. If model is slow, close other tabs.
